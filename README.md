## Description

**WALLET** is a personal finance management mobile application that empowers users to take full control over their money. Whether you're budgeting, monitoring savings, or analyzing spending habits, WALLET provides an effortless, intuitive experience with:

- Real-time transactions & insights
- Smart, secure data storage
- Powerful analytics for better financial decisions

## Key Features

### 1. Income & Expense Management
- Add, edit, and delete income or expense entries.
- Categorize transactions (Food, Transport, Bills, Entertainment, etc.).
- Define custom titles, amounts, and dates for every transaction.

### 2. Category-Wise Spending Insights
- Visual breakdown (charts, graphs) of all expenses.
- View daily, weekly, and monthly spending by category.

### 3. Monthly Budget Setup
- Set and customize monthly budget goals.
- Instant alerts when nearing or exceeding your budget.

### 4. Smart Data Persistence
- Save currency preferences and budget settings using `SharedPreferences`.
- Transaction history persists even after restarting the app.

### 5. Backup & Restore
- Export your data as text or JSON for secure backup.
- Restore your transaction history any time via internal storage.

### 6. Notifications & Alerts
- Daily reminders to log expenses.
- Monthly limit alerts to avoid overspending.

## How WALLET Works

1. **Set up your wallet**: Add your monthly budget and preferred currency.
2. **Log income & expenses**: Track your money in a few taps.
3. **Monitor your budget**: Get real-time insights and alerts.
4. **Backup your data**: Secure your financial history.
5. **Stay organized**: Improve your habits and start saving smarter.
